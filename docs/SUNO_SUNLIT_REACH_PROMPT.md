# Suno prompt: The Sunlit Reach

Use **Instrumental** mode. If Suno exposes a lyrics field, enter
`[Instrumental]`.

## Style prompt

Instrumental open-world exploration music for a luminous Japanese alpine
valley after a difficult boss victory, written for a low-poly Nintendo 3DS
action-RPG. Hopeful, peaceful, windswept, and quietly emotional rather than
triumphant. 76 BPM, spacious 4/4, Japanese pentatonic melody with an
unhurried seamless-loop feeling. Airy shakuhachi lead, delicate koto and
shamisen plucks, soft taiko pulse, occasional shinobue phrases, warm restrained
strings, low wooden percussion, and subtle natural textures suggesting river
water, mountain wind, grass, birds, and distant horse movement. Begin sparse
and intimate, open into a wider melodic section suitable for horseback travel,
then settle naturally back toward the opening harmony so the track can loop.
Clear memorable melody, generous silence, light orchestration, no combat
intensity. Instrumental only; no vocals, chanting, EDM, trap drums, distorted
guitars, trailer braams, or overpowering bass. Target length 3–4 minutes.

Suggested title: **River Beneath the Hikari Peaks**
